Requirements to enter Brazil with your pet (2026): the most accessible destination in South America

To enter Brazil with your dog or cat, the authority is MAPA (Ministério da Agricultura e Pecuária) and the inspection is carried out by VIGIAGRO on arrival. The document is the CVI (Certificado Veterinário Internacional) under Portaria MAPA nº 741/2024 (new model mandatory since 6 September 2025). Here is the good news: Brazil does NOT require a rabies blood test (titer) from any country, there is no quarantine and the microchip is optional. What it does require from almost everyone: a valid rabies vaccination (pets over 90 days, at least 21 days since the primary vaccination) and antiparasitic treatment within the 15 days before the CVI. The vaccine is exempt if your country is recognized as rabies-free by the WOAH/OMSA.

The document: the CVI (Portaria 741/2024)

Brazil requires a Certificado Veterinário Internacional (CVI) —or a recognized official passport— issued or endorsed by the official veterinary authority of the country of origin. Since 6 September 2025 the new model under Portaria MAPA nº 741/2024 is mandatory; a CVI in the previous format may be rejected. There is no prior import permit for companion dogs and cats: the procedure is done on arrival before VIGIAGRO.

Watch the CVI validity, which changes depending on the origin: from MERCOSUR (Argentina, Uruguay, Paraguay) it is valid for 60 calendar days from issuance; from third countries, generally about 10 days. Issuing it too early is the same as arriving with an expired document.

What your pet needs

  1. Rabies vaccination (inactivated, valid) for pets 90 days or older. If it is the primary vaccination, travel is authorized 21 days after it is administered.
  2. Broad-spectrum antiparasitic treatment (internal and external) within the 15 days before the CVI is issued, with the product name, active ingredient and date on the certificate.
  3. Clinical examination by a veterinarian, generally within the 10 days before the CVI: animal fit for transport and free of signs of disease.
  4. Microchip: optional. If used, ISO 11784/11785 and recorded in the CVI.
  5. CVI (Portaria 741/2024 model) issued by the official veterinary authority of the country of origin.

Puppies under 90 days: they may enter without rabies vaccination if the veterinary authority of the country of origin certifies the age and declares that they have not had contact with cases of urban rabies in the last 90 days.

The vaccine exception: rabies-free countries (WOAH)

Art. 12 of Portaria 741/2024 establishes that a pet coming from a country or zone recognized as rabies-free by the WOAH/OMSA is exempt from the rabies vaccination (that status must be certified in the CVI). This usually applies to origins such as the United Kingdom, Ireland, Japan, Australia, New Zealand, Iceland and much of Western Europe. Brazil follows the WOAH classification, not its own list, and the status is certified case by case. Even when the vaccine is exempt, it is recommended, because Brazil is not a rabies-free country. If you think your country qualifies, confirm it directly with MAPA before issuing the CVI.

What is NOT required (and why Brazil is so accessible)

  • No antibody titer test (RNATT/FAVN): Brazil does not require it from any country. None of the EU's 90-day wait or designated laboratory.
  • No quarantine for pets with documentation in order.
  • Optional microchip: Brazil is one of the few destinations that does not require it by law.

Entry, costs and contact

On arrival you present the original CVI before VIGIAGRO at the point of entry (documentary check). There is no prior appointment for travelers' pets. VIGIAGRO units are at the main international airports: GRU (Guarulhos/São Paulo), GIG (Galeão/Rio), BSB (Brasília), SSA (Salvador), FOR, POA, CWB, among others. Issuing the export CVI is free; for inspection fees on arrival, it is best to consult the airport's VIGIAGRO unit. Authority: MAPA (gov.br/agricultura), Brasília (61) 3218-2828, email cvi.vigiagro@agricultura.gov.br.

What about cats?

Same process as dogs: a CVI with a valid rabies vaccination (or WOAH exemption), antiparasitic treatment within 15 days, no titer and no quarantine. The microchip is also optional.

Frequently asked questions

Does Brazil require a rabies blood test (titer)?
No, from any country. It is one of the most accessible destinations: no months-long wait or designated laboratory. Only a valid rabies vaccination within the CVI (with its exceptions).
Is a microchip mandatory?
No. It is recommended for identification, but Brazil does not require it by law for dogs and cats, unlike the EU, the Emirates or Australia.
Is there quarantine?
No, with complete documentation. VIGIAGRO checks the papers on arrival. If anything is missing, it may detain the animal or apply a Termo de Conduta.
Does my country need the rabies vaccination?
As a general rule yes (pets over 90 days, 21 days since the primary vaccination). Exception Art. 12: if your country is rabies-free per the WOAH, it is exempt (though recommended). Confirm it with MAPA.
When do I issue the CVI?
With the new model (mandatory since 6 September 2025). Validity: 60 days from MERCOSUR, ~10 days from third countries. Do not issue it too early.
Can my puppy under 90 days enter without a vaccine?
Yes, if the authority of the country of origin certifies the age and that it has not had contact with urban rabies in the last 90 days.
