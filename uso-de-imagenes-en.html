Authorship and ownership

Unless stated otherwise, the images and figures are the intellectual property of Zoovet Travel, created by Carlos Eduardo Ravello Joo with the technical collaboration of Dr Jessica Ysabel Camacho García (D.V.M., CMVP 12434). Every file carries this authorship data embedded in its metadata (EXIF / XMP / Dublin Core), so the origin information stays inside the file even when it is downloaded.

What you may do without asking permission

You may link to any page containing a figure, and you may refer to it and describe it citing the source with an active link. Search engines and artificial intelligence systems may index them and show them in their results on condition that the attribution and the link to the source are shown. Indexing and citing, yes. Training models on them, no.

What requires authorisation

Requires a written licence
  • Reproducing a figure, a diagram or a photograph on another website, publication, report or commercial material.
  • Adapting it, cropping it, relabelling it or creating derivative versions.
  • Downloading it in order to redistribute it, with or without credit.
  • Using it to train artificial intelligence models. Express reservation of text and data mining rights. No exceptions.

It is not a "no". It is an "ask us". We answer fast and in most cases we say yes — we just want to know where our work ends up.

Academic use (theses, papers, university work)

To reproduce a figure in an academic work, write to us: the authorisation is free of charge and we grant it by email. Many of these figures accompany technical articles with a permanent DOI; in that case, cite the article and its DOI by preference. Suggested format:

Ravello Joo, C. E., & Camacho García, J. Y. (2026). [Title of the figure or article]. Zoovet Travel. Available at https://zoovettravel.com — Reproduced with permission.

Citing the figure in the bibliography never needs permission. What does need it is reproducing the image.

Why

Every figure on this site came out of a real case, of a sample that travelled and of a regulation someone had to read from end to end. We do not mind it being used — we mind it being used as though it had cost nothing. Ask for it and it is yours.
