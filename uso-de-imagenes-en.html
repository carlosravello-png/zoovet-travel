Image use and licensing

The scientific figures, diagrams and images published on Zoovet Travel are our own work and are available under Creative Commons. You may use them — we only ask that you credit us properly. The photographs in our real-story section are an exception, and we explain why below.

Licence: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)
You are free to share and adapt the material for any purpose, even commercially, as long as you give appropriate credit, link to the licence and indicate if changes were made. Legal text: creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0.

Authorship and ownership

Unless otherwise stated, the images and figures are the intellectual property of Zoovet Travel, created by Carlos Eduardo Ravello Joo with the technical collaboration of Dr. Jessica Ysabel Camacho García (DVM, CMVP 12434). Every file carries this authorship information embedded in its metadata (Dublin Core / RDF), so the origin data stays inside the file even after it is downloaded.

How to attribute (general use)

When reusing a figure, include a credit line next to it, like this:

Figure by Zoovet Travel (C. E. Ravello Joo & J. Y. Camacho García), licensed under CC BY 4.0. Source: https://zoovettravel.com

Where possible, link the credit line to the original page where the figure appears.

Academic use (theses, papers, university work)

If you are reproducing a figure in academic work, we would appreciate a citation. Many of these figures accompany technical articles with a permanent DOI; where that is the case, please cite the article and its DOI. Suggested format:

Ravello Joo, C. E., & Camacho García, J. Y. (2026). [Title of the figure or article]. Zoovet Travel. Available at https://zoovettravel.com — Licensed under CC BY 4.0.
  • State the title of the figure (you will find it in the file metadata or in the figure caption).
  • When the figure belongs to an article with a DOI, add the DOI to your bibliography.
  • You do not need to ask permission for academic use: correct attribution is enough.

Exception: photographs from the real-story section

All rights reserved — CC BY 4.0 does not apply
The photographs published in the Real travel stories section (/historias/) are excluded from the Creative Commons licence.

They are © 2026 Zoovet Travel and are published with the express authorization of the pets' owners. They show identifiable people and real clients' companion animals, and therefore may not be reproduced, adapted or reused — commercially or non-commercially — without prior written permission from Zoovet Travel and, where applicable, from the pet's owner.

Zoovet Travel is the entity that produces, curates and publishes this documentary archive of real cases; part of the visual material was contributed by the owners themselves and is published under that authorization. Every file carries authorship, ownership and copyright notice embedded in its metadata (EXIF / XMP).

If you need to license one of these images for a specific use (press, research, outreach), write to us and we will assess it case by case.

Other uses

For uses requiring terms other than CC BY 4.0 (for example, exclusivity, removal of the credit line, or bespoke material), write to us and we will arrange it.
