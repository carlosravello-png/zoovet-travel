Export medical services

What your pet needs to travel abroad

Taking your pet to another country isn't a one-day errand: it's a medical and documentary process with deadlines that can't be improvised. A test done outside its valid window can delay the trip by months. This is what we do so that doesn't happen to you.

Rabies blood test

Many destinations require a blood test proving that the rabies vaccine truly protects your pet. We draw the sample and coordinate it with an approved laboratory; that result is what starts the clock on the trip.

See details →

Health and vaccination certificate

The official document that travels with your pet. It's issued by an authorized veterinarian, within the framework regulated by the State through SENASA, after verifying microchip, vaccination and clinical condition.

See details →

Assessment packages tailored to the patient

Not every patient needs the same thing. Based on your pet's breed, age and species, we put together the right assessment before approving the trip.

Pre-flight cardio-respiratory assessment

For short-nosed (brachycephalic) breeds: Bulldog, Pug, Shih Tzu, Boston Terrier, Persians and others. They breathe differently and flying demands more of them, so we assess their heart and breathing to prepare them and keep them safe on the journey.

Frequently asked questions

What people ask us most

Do you only see patients in Trujillo?

You don't need to live in Trujillo. Besides our own branch, we have a Network of affiliated veterinarians in different regions of Peru —licensed and accredited, with proven experience, excellent client care and proper facilities, with sound cold-chain and laboratory handling— who coordinate with us under Zoovet Travel's methodology and quality control. Message us and we'll tell you how it works for your city.

Do you issue the export certificate?

Yes, within the framework regulated by SENASA and the Colegio Médico Veterinario del Perú. It's issued by an authorized veterinarian after verifying the microchip, vaccination status and clinical condition of your pet. It's not a document you can buy or fast-track by skipping requirements. You can see the details in our export certificate service and in the guide to the SENASA animal health certificate in Trujillo.

My pet is short-nosed (brachycephalic) — can it travel?

It depends on their cardio-respiratory assessment. Breeds like the Bulldog, Pug, Shih Tzu or Persian need special care in flight. We carry out the assessment —heart, breathing and lab work— to guide you with data and, if your pet needs any preparation before traveling safely, to point it out in time. To go deeper with evidence, we've published —in three languages, for colleagues, the scientific community and the public— our pre-flight assessment service, the technical study on the air transport of brachycephalic breeds and popular guides such as traveling with a pug and can my French bulldog fly?.

How far in advance should I start the process?

It depends on the destination. For the European Union and several countries in Asia, the minimum is usually 3 months because of the wait for the rabies blood test result. For Australia and New Zealand, 6 months or more. Message us as soon as you have the country and the approximate travel date. We explain it with the real timelines in the rabies blood test and in how far in advance to start the trip.

The information on this page is general in nature. Export requirements change without notice and vary by destination and species; always verify them with the competent authority and with our team before traveling.
