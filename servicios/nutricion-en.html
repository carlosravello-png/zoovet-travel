What it is and why it matters

Food is not just fuel: it is also medicine. The right diet prevents diseases, helps to manage weight and accompanies conditions such as renal, hepatic or digestive problems. And "just any food" is not the same: nutrition is tailored to each pet.

What we do

We assess your pet's body condition with the WSAVA international standard and build a diet and portion plan with clear, measurable and sustainable goals, with follow-up to adjust along the way.

What we work on (with clear goals)

  • Body condition assessment — the WSAVA method to know, with sound criteria, whether your pet is overweight, underweight or at its ideal point.
  • Weight and obesity management — a realistic and measurable plan, because obesity shortens life and worsens other diseases.
  • Diets for conditions — renal, hepatic, gastrointestinal, or to support the skin and coat, according to the diagnosis.

We do not promise miracles or fad diets. We promise a concrete plan, with measurable goals and follow-up, that your pet can sustain.

When to consult

If your pet is overweight or underweight, has difficult digestion, skin problems, or a chronic disease where diet helps. Also at every life stage, because needs change. Write to us and we will build its plan.

Is your pet overweight, having difficult digestion, or living with a disease that diet can support? Leave with a concrete plan, not with guesses. Write to us.

Frequently asked questions

How do I know if my pet is overweight?

Looking at it is not enough: it is assessed with an objective method, the WSAVA body condition score, which combines what is seen with what is felt on palpation. We apply it during the consultation and, if needed, build a realistic weight-management plan with follow-up.

Can food help with a disease?

Yes. In renal, hepatic and digestive diseases, or in obesity, the right diet is part of the treatment, not an extra. That is why we tailor the feeding to each pet's diagnosis, with measurable goals.
