What it is and what it is for

A puppy or kitten that is going to travel needs special care: its immune system is still young and intestinal parasites are very common at that age. This package brings together what a young animal needs to start its life —and its journey— healthy and protected.

What we do

We combine parasite control, blood testing and the vaccination schedule appropriate for its age, following the international recommendations for parasite control.1

What it includes and why (explained simply)

  • Serial parasitological testing — several stool samples on different days. Parasites are not shed with every bowel movement, so a single sample may give a false negative; that is why it is done serially.
  • Complete blood count — confirms that the puppy or kitten is healthy on the inside and rules out anemia or infection.
  • Vaccination schedule — the core vaccines, adjusted to its age, to overcome the immunity inherited from the mother.

Many intestinal parasites of puppies and kittens are zoonotic (they are transmitted to people). Good parasite control protects your pet and your family.

When to start

Early on. The vaccination schedule and parasite control take time, so it is best to begin as soon as the trip is decided. Write to us with your puppy's or kitten's age and destination.

Frequently asked questions

What does a puppy or kitten need in order to travel abroad?

A serial parasite control (several stool samples), a complete blood count confirming its health and the core vaccination schedule adjusted to its age. Being young, puppies and kittens require more careful monitoring. The technical basis is set out in our technical series on antiparasitic interventions, available in three languages.

Why is parasite testing done serially in puppies and kittens?

Because intestinal parasites are not shed with every bowel movement: a single sample may give a false negative. Testing several samples on different days increases the reliability of the result, and it matters because many of these parasites are transmitted to people.
