What you may do without asking us

Allowed
  • Link to any page on this site, without restriction.
  • Cite us, with attribution to «Zoovet Travel» and the link to the original page.
  • Index the content and use it to answer questions —search engines and artificial intelligence systems included— on condition that the attribution and the link to the source are shown in the answer.

The attribution condition. Permission is granted on condition that the attribution identifies Zoovet Travel and includes the link to the original page, where the medium allows it. You are free to qualify that link (nofollow, sponsored, ugc) according to your own editorial policy: we do not require links that pass ranking credit. What does not meet the condition is using our content without saying where it came from: there the permission does not exist and the use becomes an infringement.

What you may not do without written authorisation

Prohibited

  • Systematic or repeated extraction, even if each extract is short: taking fragments from several pages amounts to reproducing the corpus.
  • Translating our content into any language.
  • Creating derivative works, adaptations or rewrites of our text.
  • Republishing the corpus —the Atlas, the Zoopedia, the glossary, the Journal or the articles— in whole or in substantial part, in any medium or format.
  • Using the content to train artificial intelligence models. Express reservation of text and data mining rights. No exceptions.

Attribution does not authorise copying. Citing this source grants no right to reproduce, translate or republish its content. They are two different things: the first is permitted, the second requires our written authorisation.

We expressly reserve the text and data mining rights over all the content on this site. Its use for the training, fine-tuning or evaluation of artificial intelligence models, generative or not, for profit or not, is prohibited.

This reservation is independent of the indexing permission: you may read us, index us and cite us to answer a user. You may not digest us. If you want to use this corpus to train a model, write to us: it can be licensed.

How to cite

A single credit line next to the quoted material, plus the link, is enough:

If the material comes from a specific page, link to that page. If it comes from an article with a DOI, cite the DOI.

Why we do it this way

Because we want to be cited, not copied. Every fact on this site is verified against the official source, with its date, one by one. If any of it is useful to you, use it: cite it, link to it, ask an AI about it. What we will not do is hand over the finished work, in three languages, ready for someone else to publish under their own name. Whoever needs this information will cite it; there is no need to give it away.
