History

Zoovet Travel was founded in May 2013 in Trujillo, La Libertad region, Peru, by Carlos Eduardo Ravello Joo. The goal was to address a very specific need: guiding Peruvian families through the international pet export process — a technically complex procedure involving multiple health authorities, strict timelines and country-specific documentation.

From the outset, the clinic operated under the standards of Peru's National Agricultural Health Service (SENASA) and the Peruvian Veterinary Medical Association (CMVP). Over time, it expanded into specialty veterinary medicine, incorporating dermatology, ophthalmology, cardiology and endocrinology, all under the direction of board-certified professionals.

In over 12 years of operation, Zoovet Travel has managed the export of hundreds of pets to destinations including the United States, Spain, Italy, France, Japan, Australia, Canada, Chile and more. The clinic is located at Calle Cuba 241, Urb. El Recreo, Trujillo, and operates Monday through Saturday from 09:00 to 19:00.
