History

Zoovet Travel was founded in May 2013 in Trujillo, La Libertad region, Peru, by Carlos Eduardo Ravello Joo and Dr. Jessica Ysabel Camacho Garcia, with a very specific goal: to build a pet export service that was genuinely comprehensive — not just paperwork, but clinically backed from the first step to the last.

The commitment was clear from day one: an export process is only serious if the veterinarian signing the health certificate is also the specialist who examined the animal, who understands international zoosanitary legislation — which changes without notice and without mercy — and who holds her credentials in good standing to sign with legal validity before any veterinary authority in the world. In an industry where that critical moment is too often treated as a formality, Zoovet chose to build it for what it truly is: a medical act with real consequences. Dr. Camacho, specialist in internal medicine, clinical diagnostics, dermatology, endocrinology and veterinary nutrition, is the clinical guarantee behind every case file that leaves this clinic.

In over 12 years of operation, Zoovet Travel has managed the export of hundreds of pets to destinations including the United States, Spain, Italy, France, Japan, Australia, Canada, Chile and more, always operating under SENASA and CMVP standards. The clinic is located at Calle Cuba 2
