What we manage

Everything your pet needs to fly

Every item is a real requirement. We coordinate it all.

🔬
ISO 11784/11785 Microchip
Mandatory international identification. Must be implanted before any rabies vaccine valid for export. Without a microchip, the file has no foundation.
💉
Complete vaccination schedule
Valid rabies vaccine plus schedule by species. Dogs: parvovirus, distemper, hepatitis, leptospirosis. Cats: panleukopenia. All documented in the official booklet.
🧪
FAVN/RNATT rabies serology
Required for the EU, UK, Japan, Australia and others. Blood test confirming rabies antibodies ≥ 0.5 IU/mL. We coordinate shipping to the OIE/WOAH-accredited laboratory.
📋
SENASA Sanitary Certificate
The official document authorising the legal departure of your pet from Peru. Signed by the licensed veterinarian. Short validity — we coordinate the exact date with your flight.
📦
Certified IATA kennel
The container must comply with IATA standards by airline, route and pet size. An incorrect kennel can prevent boarding. We verify or supply the right one.
🌍
Destination-specific documentation
CDC Form for the USA, APHIS endorsement, import permit for Australia, EU customs declaration. Each country has its specifics — we manage the complete file. View requirements by destination →

Frequently asked questions

What people ask before starting

How far in advance should I start the process?

It depends on the destination. For some Latin American countries 2 to 4 weeks may be enough. For the European Union or the United Kingdom the minimum process from scratch takes 4 to 5 months — it includes vaccination, 30-day wait, blood sample for serology and 3 months counted from the extraction date. For Japan or Australia it can exceed 6 months. Write to us first — we'll tell you exactly how much time you have.

Can you manage the process from any city in Peru?

Yes. We coordinate files from Lima, Arequipa, Cusco, Piura, Chiclayo, Trujillo and any other city in Peru. SENASA procedures and lab sample shipping are coordinated remotely. The clinical examination is performed at our clinic or we coordinate with licensed veterinarians in your area.

Does my pet need serology to travel to the USA?

Under CDC 2024 regulations, Peru is classified as a high-risk country for canine rabies. Dogs must present a rabies antibody test (RNATT/FAVN) with a result ≥ 0.5 IU/mL from an accredited laboratory, plus an ISO microchip. CDC regulations change — we always verify the current version before starting your file. What happens if you travel without FAVN? →

What happens if something fails at the destination airport?

If the documentation is incomplete or incorrect, the animal can be detained, subjected to mandatory quarantine at the owner's expense, or returned. That is why we audit each file before the flight. In 12 years of real management, we have had no rejections due to deficient documentation on files we managed from start to finish.

Do cats and dogs have the same process?

Not exactly. Vaccination schedules differ and some destinations have specific requirements for cats. Rabies serology applies to both species when the destination requires it. We evaluate each case separately — write to us with your pet's details.

How much does managing the entire process cost?

The cost varies by destination, starting point of your pet's file and services required. We do not quote without understanding the case — every file is different. Write to us on WhatsApp with the destination and your pet's current situation and we will give you a free assessment.
