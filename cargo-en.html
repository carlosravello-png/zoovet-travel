The Clinical Differentiator

Exporting a pet is not a paperwork exercise. It is a medical process with real consequences for the animal's health — and that requires the person who signs the health certificate to also be the one who clinically evaluated the animal, knows its history, respiratory condition, vaccination status, and specific risk factors.

Our veterinary team, registered with the CMVP (No. 12434) and authorized by SENASA, performs a complete clinical evaluation of every animal before any document is issued. We do not outsource the medical side — it is the core of our service.

International zoosanitary regulations change without prior notice. A requirement that was valid for Spain six months ago may have been amended. Our team actively monitors those changes — it is part of the work, not an additional consultation.

Spain

  • ISO 11784/11785 microchip implanted before rabies vaccination
  • Valid rabies vaccine with booster as per manufacturer's protocol
  • Official SENASA/TRACES zoosanitary certificate, issued no more than 10 days before the flight

European Union

France

  • ISO 11784/11785 microchip implanted before the first rabies vaccination
  • Valid rabies vaccine recorded in veterinary passport
  • Official TRACES certificate issued by SENASA competent authority

Estimated time: 3–6 weeks

European Union

Germany

  • ISO 11784/11785 microchip registered before rabies vaccine
  • Valid rabies vaccine in accordance with manufacturer's protocol
  • Official TRACES/SENASA health certificate with exit veterinary inspection

Estimated time: 3–6 weeks

Middle East

United Arab Emirates

  • ISO 11784/11785 microchip and up-to-date rabies vaccine with booster
  • Rabies serology (RNATT/FAVN) with titre ≥ 0.5 IU/ml in an OIE-authorized laboratory
  • Prior import permit issued by MOCCAE (UAE Ministry of Climate Change and Environment)

Estimated time: 3–5 months

Asia Pacific

Japan

  • ISO microchip + 2 FAVN serology tests at least 30 days apart, both with titre ≥ 0.5 IU/ml
  • Minimum waiting period of 180 days from the second positive serology before the flight
  • Prior notification to Japan's Ministry of Agriculture (MAFF) and quarantine of up to 180 days upon arrival if the protocol is not completed correctly

Estimated time: 8–12 months minimum

Full guide →

Oceania

Australia

  • ISO microchip + valid rabies vaccine + FAVN serology with titre ≥ 0.5 IU/ml
  • Antiparasitic treatment (Praziquantel) and internal deworming within specific pre-flight timeframes
  • Mandatory quarantine upon arrival (minimum 10 days at a DAFF-approved facility)

Estimated time: 6–10 months

Europe — Post Brexit

United Kingdom

  • ISO 11784/11785 microchip implanted before the rabies vaccine
  • Rabies vaccine with a 21-day waiting period from primary vaccination before travel
  • Antiparasitic treatment with praziquantel applied between 24 and 120 hours before departure (dogs only)

Estimated time: 4–8 weeks

The 5-Step Process

1

Diagnostic consultation

We assess the destination, species, breed, current vaccination status, and the animal's risk factors. Here we determine whether the trip is viable, which protocol applies, how long it takes, and what it costs. No commitment to hire.

2

Clinical protocols

ISO 11784/11785 microchip implantation, vaccine schedule update, internal and external deworming, and rabies serology RNATT/FAVN when required by the destination. All performed by our veterinary team within the correct regulatory timelines.

3

SENASA management

We process the Export Zoosanitary Certificate (EZC) with SENASA, with issuance timelines coordinated to the confirmed flight. The certificate has a limited validity window — issuance outside that window invalidates the shipment.

4

Logistics coordination

We advise on the correct IATA travel crate for the animal's size and weight, airline requirements, and boarding documentation. We verify the complete file is in order before airport check-in.

5

Post-departure follow-up

We are available to answer questions during transit and guide you through the entry process in the destination country. If quarantine is required, we explain how it works and what to expect.

Completed Shipments

Pets that have arrived at their destinations with a complete protocol and without issues. Every case is unique — every file, audited.
