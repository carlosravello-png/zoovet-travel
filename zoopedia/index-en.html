The Zoopedia of International Pet Export and Import

International Guide to Official Requirements by Country — Developed and Verified by Zoovet Travel (Permanent Update 2026)

Check official requirements to import and export pets by country. Vaccines, microchip, RNATT, health certificates, quarantine and regulations updated 2026. Editorial project developed and verified by Zoovet Travel.

United States

CDC 2024 requirements for dog and cat import from Peru and Latin America. Final Rule, RNATT, ACF.

Japan

AQS 2026 requirements for dog and cat import to Japan from Peru and Latin America. Model A, 180-day post-RNATT wait.

United Arab Emirates

MOCCAE 2026 requirements to import dogs and cats to UAE. Banned breeds, Manifest Cargo, 90-day wait.

Spain

Complete MAPA and EU 2026 requirements to bring pets to Spain from Peru. SENASA certification, RNATT, EU approved labs.

Italy

Ministero della Salute and EU 2026 requirements for pet import to Italy. SENASA, RNATT, 90 days from sample. No EU lab in Peru.

France

Ministère de l'Agriculture and EU 2026 requirements for pet import to France. SENASA, RNATT, 90 days. No EU lab in Peru.

Australia

Peru and Latin America are DAFF non-approved countries. Direct travel impossible: intermediate country required, minimum 12 months, Mickleham quarantine Melbourne.

New Zealand

Peru and Latin America are MPI unapproved countries. Direct import impossible: 6-month intermediate country, RNATT 90 days, 10-day quarantine Auckland or Christchurch.

European Union (base requirements)

EU 2026 requirements for any member state from non-listed countries. SENASA, RNATT, 90-day wait. Directory of 27 authorities. Finland, Ireland, Malta, Norway: mandatory antiparasitic treatment.

United Kingdom (Great Britain and Northern Ireland)

APHA 2026 post-Brexit requirements. Great Britain Pet Health Certificate differs from EU model. SENASA, RNATT, mandatory Praziquantel. London vs Belfast: different certificates.

South Korea (Republic of Korea)

No post-RNATT waiting period: process from Peru in 45 days. SENASA, APQA, WOAH labs. Shortest process in Asia. Incheon, microchip control.

Chile

No RNATT: process from Peru in 10 days. SAG, Resolution 6.052/2023, Echinococcus antiparasitic and digital declaration ingresoachile.cl.

Canada

No RNATT: process from Peru in 5 days. SENASA bilingual certificate, CFIA, Health of Animals Regulations. Ontario breed alert.

Brazil

No RNATT: process from Peru in 10 days. SENASA CVI MERCOSUR model, antiparasitic 15 days before CVI issuance, VIGIAGRO Guarulhos.

China (P.R.)

No post-RNATT wait, 45 days from scratch. SENASA, GACC, GACC-accredited lab, 30-day quarantine for non-compliance, one pet per passport.

Russia

No RNATT for any country: 22 days from scratch. SENASA CVI Form 5a, 20-day post-vaccine wait, annual Rosselkhoznadzor criterion, 5-day window.

South Africa

75-90 days from scratch. Mandatory 14-day quarantine, prior DALRRD Import Permit, Manifest Cargo only. SENASA, RNATT, Leptospirosis two strains.

Singapore

Category D: 6-month process, mandatory 30-day SAQS quarantine, RNATT, antiparasitic 2-7 days, Manifest Cargo. SENASA.

India

No RNATT: 45 days from scratch. AQCS NOC 15 days prior, redundant microchip in SENASA certificate, antiparasitic 2-7 days. Six authorised airports.
