How does the traffic light work?

Green

More than 90 days to travel and all destination requirements met with correct order and deadlines.

Amber

Between 30 and 90 days, or vaccine in the grey zone (21-29 days). Left to the officer's discretion — in practice almost always NO.

Red

Blocker impossible to resolve within the timeframe: insufficient age, FAVN absent with little time, quarantine not booked.

The traffic light engine evaluates coherence between three axes: biological age and clinical status (E), documentation and regulations (C) and destination + travel date (P). Designed by Carlos Eduardo Ravello Joo.

Frequently asked questions

What exactly does this tool calculate? ▼

The Planner computes your pet's reverse export timeline: legal minimum age by destination, rabies vaccine deadline, FAVN/titer test window, health certificate validity, SENASA endorsement and destination country permits/quarantines. It returns a viability traffic light and a full PDF plan.

Why require 30 days for the vaccine if guides say 21? ▼

Legally most destinations accept 21 days, but SENASA Peru and variable officer criteria make 30 days the recommended minimum to avoid rejection. The amber light (vaccine 21-29 days) implies officer's discretion — in practice almost always NO.

Why is the health certificate only valid for 5 days? ▼

SENASA Peru, as the issuing authority, only validates the health certificate for 5 real days, regardless of what the destination country guide states. Without SENASA validity there is no CZE endorsement and the pet cannot travel.

What do I do if I get a red light? ▼

If the traffic light is red, contact us via WhatsApp at +51 922 083 707. There are still options — postpone date, change destination, adjust plan. Don't make decisions without us.
