Where to process the pet travel certificate in Trujillo

Technical guide by Dr Jessica Camacho on official offices and procedures to process the pet export certificate in Trujillo, Peru.

Jessica Ysabel Camacho Garcia, DVM — CMVP 12434 — Zoovet Travel, Trujillo, Peru  |  February 2026
Scope statement — required reading This article is a descriptive technical document. It is not legal or individualized veterinary advice. It does not replace official regulations of any jurisdiction nor the assessment of the responsible veterinarian.

Requirements vary by country, route, species and airline. Regulations change frequently. Verification with the competent health authority of the destination and transit country is mandatory before any export process.

Certificate issuance is the sole responsibility of the authorized veterinarian. This article does not override that professional judgment.

Obtaining the Official Veterinary Export Certificate (CZE) in La Libertad is not an automatic process depending on the owner's will but on the physical validation of an official SENASA inspector. Understanding where to process the pet travel certificate in Trujillo prevents clinical examination validity periods from expiring before the final endorsement. In practice the most common error is trying to manage documents on the day of the flight, ignoring that the health authority requires non-negotiable review lead times.

Section 1 Official offices and administrative jurisdiction in La Libertad

The final inspection and certificate issuance is centralised at the SENASA office located at the Services Complex on the Panamericana Norte highway. It is at this office that the documentary chain is verified, including the health certificate issued by the registered veterinarian and valid vaccination records. What people do not anticipate is that the inspector must perform an in-person microchip reading; if the transponder is not detectable or does not comply with ISO 11784/11785, the process stops immediately with no right of appeal.

Document traceability depends on correct foliation of private health certificates that serve as the basis for the State information system. In Trujillo the airport cargo terminal office only acts as a final verification point, not as a centre for issuing new files, a detail that causes flight losses every week.

Section 2 Prerequisites for counter service

Before attending the official authority, the animal must have undergone a thorough clinical examination by an authorised private veterinarian certifying absence of signs of infectious-contagious disease. This examination has a validity that is usually a maximum of 10 days for most destinations but can be reduced to 48 hours if the receiving country so requires. Presentation of the payment voucher for processing fees at the Banco de la Nación is mandatory and must match the tax code for companion animal export.

The owner must present the original vaccination record, where the rabies vaccination compliant with international movement regulations must be valid for more than 21 days and less than one year from application. Any erasure or amendment on the physical document will be grounds for rejection by the inspector on duty in Trujillo. Documentary security is the priority of the Peruvian State to prevent export of zoonoses; consistency between microchip date and vaccination date is the first audit point of the La Libertad regional office.

Section 3 Role of the registered veterinarian in Trujillo

The private veterinarian's role in this process is to act as the first sanitary filter and manager of the file that will reach the official inspector. In our Trujillo practice we enter data into the VUCE (Single Window for Foreign Trade) system to streamline document review before the physical appointment. This technical step reduces the probability of typographical errors on the CZE, which are extremely difficult to correct once the document has been printed and stamped by the government authority.

Coordination between the clinic and SENASA ensures the animal does not suffer prolonged waits in public offices, minimising thermal and behavioural stress. A well-prepared file from the private sector flows quickly through state bureaucracy, while a folder with informational gaps ends in a re-inspection order or travel denial. Efficiency in knowing where to process the pet travel certificate in Trujillo lies in preparing the entire medical basis weeks before the boarding deadline.

Section 4 What to resolve before starting

Verification of the destination country's import requirements must be done before scheduling any appointment in Trujillo or Lima offices. This changed in August 2024 for certain international destinations without prior notice, so relying on previous years' travellers' experiences is a critical risk. At Zoovet Travel we audit the destination technical sheet to confirm whether a period of home quarantine or additional laboratory tests is required before issuing the local health certificate.

Payment of the corresponding fees must be made in the exact name of the owner on the flight ticket to avoid discrepancies in CZE ownership. SENASA offices in Trujillo operate on strict administrative hours and do not perform inspections outside their jurisdiction without duly supported prior scheduling. Transfer logistics to the inspection office must consider Trujillo's ambient temperature, avoiding peak solar radiation hours so as not to alter the animal's physiological parameters during the inspector's review.

Document digitisation is now a requirement that complements the physical file for international pet traceability. Keeping readable copies of serology results when required by the destination, and of internal and external parasite treatment certificates, ensures a quick response to any query at flight stops. Knowing where to process the pet travel certificate in Trujillo is only the start; the technical execution of the process is what guarantees your pet crosses the border without sanitary setbacks.

An error in managing the official veterinary export certificate will result in your pet being held at customs and total loss of your logistical investment.
