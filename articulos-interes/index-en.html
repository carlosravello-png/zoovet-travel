Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between these guides and the scientific articles in the technical series?

The scientific articles delve into the clinical and regulatory basis of each topic — serology, quarantine, health certificates, microchip. The practical guides are action-oriented: what to do, in what order, what documents to obtain and how to avoid common mistakes during the export process. They are complementary and you can consult both depending on your needs.

Is the information up to date with current regulations?

Yes. Each guide is prepared with the regulations in force at the time of publication and is reviewed when relevant regulatory changes occur. For active procedures, we always recommend checking with our clinic, as pet import regulations can change without notice, especially for destinations like the United States, the European Union, or Australia.

Do the guides apply to any destination country?

There are general procedure guides — applicable to most international exports — and specific guides by country or type of procedure. You can search by your destination or by the procedure you need: microchip, deworming, flight stress, health certificate, among others.

Can I share these guides with my local veterinarian or bring them to the airport?

Yes, they are designed to be consulted by owners and veterinary teams in any format. However, for official documents — health certificates, import permits, laboratory results — you must always use the originals issued by the competent authorities. The guides are orientation material, not substitute documents.
