Directory of airlines with a pet cargo (live animals / AVI) program

Which airlines move your pet as cargo (live animals / AVI), how to book with each one, and what the official source is. Cargo only: in-cabin and checked baggage are out of scope.

Last verified: 10 June 2026 · 12 airlines · source: official cargo websites.

Of the 24 airlines with a live-animal (AVI) cargo program verified on their official websites, 13 accept bookings only through a certified cargo agent (LATAM, Avianca, Air France KLM Martinair, Lufthansa, IAG, Air Canada, Qatar, Turkish, Cathay, Korean, Swiss, Singapore and ANA), 9 allow booking directly with the airline (Copa, Aeroméxico, Emirates, American, Etihad, Qantas, Virgin Atlantic, Ethiopian and China Airlines), and 2 keep the program but not for the general public: United (PetSafe) and Delta only accept pets as cargo from active U.S. military and State Department personnel. In-cabin and checked-baggage rules are documented everywhere; this directory compiles what did not exist in one source: which airlines move pets as cargo and how to book.

🟦 Only via a certified cargo agent

🇨🇱 LATAM Cargo (LA) Agent only

LATAM Cargo moves your pet as cargo through its ALIVE program; the international booking is made only through a certified cargo agent, with its hub in Lima.

Program
ALIVE (live animals), combinable with the EXP service; segregated areas with controlled temperature and oxygenation and top boarding priority.
Booking
All international bookings for pets must be made through a certified cargo agent (freight forwarder).
Coverage
LATAM passenger and freighter network. Hub for Peru: Lima (LIM). See the departure authority in Peru (SENASA).
Contact
Pet emergencies: petsemergency@latam.com
Source
latamcargo.com/alive · verified 10 Jun 2026

🇨🇴 Avianca Cargo (AV) Agent only

Avianca Cargo ships pets as cargo within its Special Shipments; the operation is agent/shipper oriented, with its hub in Bogotá.

Program
Live Animals product within Special Shipments / Special Products; handled per IATA LAR.
Booking
Agent/shipper-oriented operation: the agent or shipper verifies requirements and handles the shipment.
Coverage
Hub Bogotá (BOG); secondary Medellín (JMC) and Miami (MIA). Departure from Colombia (ICA).
Restrictions
No rodents (including hamsters, guinea pigs, rabbits), no aggressive animals, none under 8 weeks, no females that gave birth in the last 48 h, no pregnant females without certificate. Brachycephalics: confirm on the current official policy.
Source
aviancacargo.com · verified 10 Jun 2026

🟥 Restricted program — not for the general public

🇺🇸 Delta Cargo (DL) Restricted

Delta Cargo keeps a pet cargo program, but restricted: only active U.S. military and State Department personnel; the general public cannot use it.

Program
Live Animals / pet shipment, with cargo facilities; it exists, but access is limited.
Booking
Until further notice, only for active U.S. military and State Department (FSO) personnel with permanent change of station (PCS) orders. The general public cannot ship pets as cargo and must use a licensed pet shipping company.
Coverage
U.S. and Delta's international network. Departure from United States (APHIS).
Source
delta.com · shipping your pet · verified 10 Jun 2026

Methodology and verification

Every data point comes exclusively from the airline's official cargo website, with its URL and verification date (10 June 2026). No agencies, forums or third parties were used. Stable fields (program, booking channel, hubs and facilities) are published on the card; volatile fields — breed lists, seasonal embargoes, fares, temperature limits and the 2024 U.S. rule — link to the live official policy instead of being fixed here, because they change often. When an official page blocks direct reading, the official page is linked so the data can be confirmed at source.

Scope. This directory covers only pet transport as cargo (live animals / AVI, under manifest or air waybill). In-cabin travel and checked baggage are deliberately out of scope: they are widely documented by almost every airline. Carriers such as Air Europa, Qatar Airways or Singapore Airlines, whose dog and cat flow is in-cabin or checked baggage rather than a separate AVI cargo program for individuals, do not form a card in this version.
