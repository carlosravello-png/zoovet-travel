Key information

What is a rabies serology test and why is it required?

A rabies serology test — also known as the RNATT (Rabies Neutralizing Antibody Titration Test) or FAVN test — is a blood test that measures the level of protective rabies antibodies in your dog or cat. The result is expressed in IU/mL (International Units per milliliter).

For your pet to enter the European Union, Spain, Italy, France, Germany, Japan, Australia and other rabies-free countries, the result must be 0.5 IU/mL or higher. Without this requirement met, the pet may be held at the destination airport or placed in mandatory quarantine — sometimes for months and at high cost.

At Zoovet Travel we manage the full protocol: from evaluating the prior vaccination schedule, through sample collection, shipment to KSVDL at Kansas State University (OIE/WOAH-accredited), result interpretation, and integration with SENASA documentation.

Our preparation protocol optimizes the vaccination schedule to maximize the immune response before the blood draw.

Step by step

The rabies serology process from Peru

1

Initial evaluation via WhatsApp

Send us your pet's details (species, breed, age, destination country, travel date) and we verify the current vaccination status. We define the exact schedule so your pet arrives at the journey with everything in order. No cost, immediate response.

2

ISO microchip + valid rabies vaccine

Your dog or cat must have an ISO 11784/11785 microchip placed before vaccination. We verify or administer the rabies vaccination with a registered export vaccine. From here the mandatory 30-day countdown begins before the blood draw.

3

Blood draw and shipment to KSVDL

At our clinic in Trujillo we collect the serum sample and ship it to the Rabies Laboratory at Kansas State University (KSVDL), an OIE/WOAH-accredited laboratory whose results are accepted in all countries requiring the rabies antibody titer. Results arrive in 7–14 business days.

4

Result, documentation and regulatory wait

If the titer is ≥ 0.5 IU/mL the result is valid. We integrate the certificate into the export documentation alongside the SENASA health certificate. For EU destinations, regulations require an additional 3-month wait from the result date before travel. We handle all follow-up with you.

Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about rabies serology

What is a rabies serology test and why is it required for pet travel?

A rabies serology test (RNATT) measures the protective rabies antibodies in your dog or cat's blood. Many countries require a titer of 0.5 IU/mL or higher before allowing your pet to enter. Without an approved result, your pet may be detained or placed in quarantine at the destination airport — one of the most critical requirements for international pet export from Peru.

Which countries require rabies serology for dogs and cats?

It is mandatory in: all EU countries (Spain, Italy, France, Germany, etc.), the UK, Japan, Australia, New Zealand and other rabies-free countries. At Zoovet Travel we use KSVDL at Kansas State University, an OIE/WOAH-accredited lab accepted in all these destinations.

How long does the rabies serology process take from Peru?

The minimum process takes 60 to 90 days from the first valid vaccination. Vaccine is applied (ISO microchip required first), 30 days wait, sample drawn and sent to KSVDL. Results in 7–14 business days. For the EU an extra 3 months from the positive result date are required before travel. Starting early is key.

Does rabies serology apply to cats as well?

Yes. The RNATT/FAVN protocol applies equally to dogs and cats. Both species are susceptible to rabies and destination countries require the titer for both. We manage the complete protocol for both at Zoovet Travel.

What happens if the serology result is below 0.5 IU/mL?

A booster vaccine is given and the test repeated after 30 days. Our preparation protocol optimizes the vaccination schedule before the blood draw. We manage the full case until a satisfactory result is obtained.

Can I manage serology from Chiclayo, Piura, Cajamarca or other northern Peru cities?

Yes. We coordinate with owners from Chiclayo, Piura, Cajamarca, Chimbote, Huaraz and other cities in northern Peru. Consultation and protocol planning via WhatsApp. The owner travels to our Trujillo clinic only for the blood draw. All follow-up is handled remotely.
