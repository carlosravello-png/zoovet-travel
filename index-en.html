Zoovet Travel | Specialized Veterinary Clinic and International Pet Export

Zoovet Travel is a specialized veterinary clinic with over 12 years of experience in international pet export and advanced medical care for dogs and cats. We operate from Trujillo, Peru, managing international pet relocation to the United States, Europe, Canada, and Latin America under strict sanitary and regulatory standards.

We are experts in exporting pets from Peru, complying with all international sanitary requirements demanded by each destination country, including ISO microchip, current rabies vaccination, serological tests, international health certificate, and SENASA procedures for pet export.

For over a decade, we have executed dog and cat export processes without documentary errors or sanitary rejections, consolidating a record of technical compliance and 100 % customer satisfaction in international pet transport.

In addition to our expertise in international pet export, we provide specialized veterinary care in:

  • Veterinary dermatology
  • Nutrition
  • Veterinary internal medicine
  • Veterinary ophthalmology
  • Endocrinology
  • Diagnostic Imaging (Ultrasound, ECG, Echocardiogram)

Our team consists of two veterinarians with clinical experience and training in international sanitary regulations, ensuring comprehensive medical evaluation and professional monitoring for local patients and for pets in the export process.

Zoovet Travel integrates specialized veterinary medicine with high-precision international logistics, positioning itself as a leader in exporting pets from Peru and as a premier veterinary clinic in Trujillo.
