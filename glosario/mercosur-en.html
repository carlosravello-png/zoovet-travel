MERCOSUR: harmonized animal-health requirements for dogs and cats

MERCOSUR defines common animal-health requirements and a single International Veterinary Certificate (CVI) model for the movement of dogs and cats between its Member States, simplifying travel compared with third countries.

Author: Carlos Eduardo Ravello Joo Clinical review: Dra. Jessica Ysabel Camacho García, CMVP 12434 Published: 25 June 2026

Definition based on documentary practice 2013-2026 · Zoovet Travel, Trujillo, Peru

Quick definition

South American bloc · harmonized CVI · common requirements

MERCOSUR (Mercado Común del Sur, the Southern Common Market) is the economic integration bloc of South America. In international pet movement, it defines harmonized animal-health requirements and a common International Veterinary Certificate (CVI) model for the movement of dogs and cats between its Member States.

Formal technical definition

MERCOSUR (Mercado Común del Sur) is the economic integration bloc of South America. In the context of international pet movement, it defines HARMONIZED ANIMAL-HEALTH requirements and a common International Veterinary Certificate (CVI) model for the movement of dogs and cats between its Member States. The rules are agreed within the Common Market Group (GMC), the bloc's executive body. The MERCOSUR-model CVI records the animal's data, vaccination status and required treatments, and is valid for 60 calendar days between Member States.

Regulatory context in pet movement

Traveling with a pet between MERCOSUR countries uses the harmonized CVI, with common requirements —a valid rabies vaccination, antiparasitic treatment and a clinical examination— which simplifies movement compared with procedures for third countries. The rules are agreed within the Common Market Group (GMC). Brazil incorporated the harmonized model through Portaria MAPA No. 741/2024, which reflects GMC Resolution No. 20/24, mandatory from 6 September 2025. The signature of the origin animal-health authority (in Peru, SENASA; in Brazil, MAPA) remains the final step before travel.

Typical timeline of a MERCOSUR movement

  • Complete the health fileValid rabies vaccination, antiparasitic treatment and microchip. The CVI only reflects what has already been done.
  • Clinical exam and CVI issuanceThe veterinarian examines the animal and the origin animal-health authority issues the MERCOSUR-model International Veterinary Certificate.
  • CVI validityThe CVI is valid for 60 calendar days between Member States. It is best to travel within that period.
  • Presentation at the borderThe CVI is presented to the veterinary border control of the destination Member State.

Common errors

  • Confusing an Associated State with a Member State. Only Argentina, Brazil, Paraguay and Uruguay are full Member States. Chile and Peru are Associated States, where specific conditions may apply.
  • Traveling with an expired CVI. The MERCOSUR-model CVI is valid for 60 calendar days. After that period, a new one must be issued.
  • Using an older certificate model in Brazil. Since 6 September 2025, the harmonized model of Portaria MAPA No. 741/2024 is mandatory.
  • Omitting a common requirement. A missing valid rabies vaccination, antiparasitic treatment or clinical examination can block movement even if the CVI is issued.

Frequently asked questions

Which countries make up MERCOSUR for pet movement purposes?
The full Member States are Argentina, Brazil, Paraguay and Uruguay, with Bolivia in the process of accession. There are also Associated States: Chile, Colombia, Ecuador, Peru, Guyana and Suriname. The harmonized International Veterinary Certificate (CVI) and common animal-health requirements apply between the Member States.
How long is the MERCOSUR-model CVI valid?
The MERCOSUR-model International Veterinary Certificate (CVI) is valid for 60 calendar days for the movement of dogs and cats between Member States. If the trip is delayed beyond that period, a new certificate must be issued.
Are Chile and Peru included in MERCOSUR?
Chile and Peru are Associated States of MERCOSUR, not full Member States. The harmonized animal-health requirements and the common CVI model apply fully between the Member States (Argentina, Brazil, Paraguay and Uruguay). Specific bilateral conditions may apply for Associated States.
Since when is the new CVI model mandatory in Brazil?
Brazil incorporated the harmonized model through Portaria MAPA No. 741/2024, which reflects GMC Resolution No. 20/24. Its use is mandatory from 6 September 2025.
What health requirements does MERCOSUR set for dogs and cats?
The common requirements include a valid rabies vaccination, antiparasitic treatment and a clinical examination, all recorded in the harmonized International Veterinary Certificate (CVI). This simplifies movement between Member States compared with procedures for third countries.
