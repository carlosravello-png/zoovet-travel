CFIA (Canadian Food Inspection Agency): Canadian authority for pet import

CFIA regulates pet import into Canada. It accepts FAVN or RFFIT ≥ 0.5 IU/mL. Certificate must be in English or French. No mandatory quarantine with complete documentation.

Author: Carlos Eduardo Ravello Joo Clinical review: Dra. Jessica Ysabel Camacho García, CMVP 12434 Published: 12 May 2026

Definition based on documentary practice 2013-2026 · Zoovet Travel, Trujillo, Peru

Quick definition

CFIA · CBSA · Perú = país con rabia · Microchip+vacuna+FAVN · Cuarentena

CFIA (Canadian Food Inspection Agency) is the Canadian federal agency regulating the import of companion animals. For dogs and cats from Peru, classified as a rabies country, CFIA requires ISO microchip, valid rabies vaccination, and rabies serology (FAVN or RFFIT) ≥ 0.5 IU/mL performed at an approved laboratory.

Formal technical definition

Canada accepts both FAVN and RFFIT for rabies serology — like Chile and unlike the EU. The health certificate must be issued by an official veterinarian of the exporting country (in Peru, authorized SENASA vet) and must be in English or French. Border inspection is carried out by CBSA (Canada Border Services Agency) which acts as the enforcement arm of CFIA regulations. There is no mandatory quarantine if documentation is complete and serology is positive.

Regulatory context in export

The Lima-Toronto or Lima-Vancouver route is relevant for the Peruvian community in Canada. The advantage over the US is that Canada does not have the additional CDC/DMRVV requirement for dogs — CFIA standards apply directly. However, since 2024 CBSA has intensified documentary controls at the border, especially for animals from countries with a rabies history like Peru.

Common errors

  • Presenting the certificate only in Spanish. CFIA requires the certificate in English or French. A SENASA certificate only in Spanish will be rejected at the Canadian border.
  • Assuming no quarantine without verifying complete documentation. If any document is missing or serology is not properly certified, CBSA may hold the animal or send it back.
  • Not verifying the entry point. Not all Canadian airports have CFIA inspectors available 24/7 for imported pets. Verify with the airline and CFIA the enabled entry point.

Frequently asked questions

Does Canada accept RFFIT in addition to FAVN?
Yes. Unlike the EU, Canada accepts both FAVN and RFFIT with result ≥ 0.5 IU/mL. Both must be performed at a CFIA-approved laboratory.
Is there mandatory quarantine on arrival in Canada with pets from Peru?
There is no mandatory quarantine if documentation is complete: microchip, vaccine, positive serology and bilingual certificate. CBSA performs documentary inspection and may conduct physical inspection.
Must the certificate be in English or can it be in French?
CFIA accepts English or French. To facilitate border processing, English is recommended for English-speaking airports (Toronto, Vancouver) and French for Montreal.
Do US CDC/DMRVV regulations apply to pets traveling via the US to Canada?
If the flight has a US layover and the pet must leave the transit zone, CDC regulations apply for entry into the US. For direct transit without leaving the international area, CDC regulations generally do not apply.
Is KSVDL a CFIA-approved laboratory?
Yes. KSVDL is on the CFIA approved laboratory list for FAVN and RFFIT analysis from countries with a rabies history like Peru.
