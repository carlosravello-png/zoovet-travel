MAPA (Spain Ministry of Agriculture): competent authority for pets imported into Spain

MAPA applies Regulation (EU) 576/2013 in Spain. To bring a pet from Peru to Spain: FAVN ≥ 0.5 IU/mL + 3-month wait + TRACES NT certificate + BCP review at Barajas.

Author: Carlos Eduardo Ravello Joo Clinical review: Dra. Jessica Ysabel Camacho García, CMVP 12434 Published: 12 May 2026

Definition based on documentary practice 2013-2026 · Zoovet Travel, Trujillo, Peru

Quick definition

MAPA · Reg. UE 576/2013 · FAVN+3 meses · TRACES NT · Punto de entrada Barajas

Spain's MAPA (Ministerio de Agricultura, Pesca y Alimentación) is the competent authority applying Regulation (EU) 576/2013 for pet import into Spanish territory. For dogs and cats from Peru, MAPA requires full compliance with the EU scheme: ISO microchip + vaccine + FAVN ≥ 0.5 IU/mL + 3-month wait + EU-format health certificate.

Formal technical definition

Spain is the main European destination for pets exported from Peru, given the large Peruvian community resident there. The main entry point is Madrid-Barajas airport (MAD), whose border control post (BCP) performs documentary and physical review of the animal. The export health certificate must be generated in the TRACES NT system (Trade Control and Expert System) by the official SENASA vet to be valid in the EU.

Regulatory context in export

Spain applies Regulation 576/2013 without additional exceptions for Peru. However, the TRACES NT procedure is a critical practical requirement: the official SENASA vet must register and export the certificate in TRACES NT before the trip. A paper certificate without a TRACES NT number may be rejected at the Barajas BCP. The Peruvian community in Madrid is the largest in Europe, making the Lima-Madrid route the most relevant of the bloc.

Common errors

  • Not registering the certificate in TRACES NT. A paper certificate without a TRACES NT number may be rejected at the Barajas BCP, even if the document is correct in content.
  • Not counting the 3 months from the FAVN result. The wait is counted from the result date, not the vaccine. The slightest calculation error can result in animal detention at the BCP.
  • Entering through an airport without a pet-enabled BCP. Not all Spanish airports have an authorized BCP for companion animals from third countries. The entry point must be an enabled BCP.

Frequently asked questions

Is the Barajas BCP available on weekends?
The Madrid-Barajas airport BCP has extended hours for animal inspection, but it is essential to notify the BCP in advance through the TRACES NT system so inspectors are available upon arrival.
What happens if the flight arrives in Barcelona instead of Madrid?
Barcelona-El Prat (BCN) also has an enabled BCP for companion animals from third countries. The TRACES NT certificate is valid for any enabled EU BCP.
Can I buy the ticket for my pet directly from the airline or do I need an agent?
You can book the hold space directly with most airlines, but it is recommended to do so at least 4-6 weeks in advance. The sanitary documentation (TRACES NT) is independent of the airline ticket.
Does MAPA have a list of authorized vets in Peru to issue the certificate?
The certificate must be issued by an official SENASA vet authorized to issue CSE. MAPA/EU does not maintain its own list of vets in Peru — the responsibility falls on SENASA.
If the pet already has EU residence (EU passport), does it need FAVN every time it travels?
No. Once in the EU with a valid European pet passport, the animal can move within the EU space without repeating FAVN. The complete process only applies to the first entry from Peru.
