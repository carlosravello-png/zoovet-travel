Serology and rabies

FAVN
Fluorescent Antibody Virus Neutralization
Rabies antibody test required to export pets to the EU, Japan, Australia and other high-standard countries.
≥ 0.5 IU/mL · OIE/WOAH · KSVDL
See full definition →
🔬 RFFIT
Rapid Fluorescent Focus Inhibition Test
RFFIT measures rabies antibodies like FAVN but is not accepted by the EU, Japan or Australia for pet importation.
Not valid EU · Not valid Japan · Not valid Australia
See full definition →
Rabies serology
Rabies antibody titration
Blood test measuring antibody level against rabies virus. FAVN is the internationally recognized methodology by OIE/WOAH.
≥ 0.5 IU/mL · OIE/WOAH
See full definition →
🦠 Rabies
Zoonotic disease of mandatory notification
The rabies virus is the ultimate reason FAVN and all rabies vaccination protocols required for pet export exist.
Lyssavirus · OIE List A · Peru: active surveillance
See full definition →

Japan · 91 days
Two doses, the first at 91 days — not 84 — and RNA vaccines are not accepted.
2 doses · from 91 days

Documents and certificates

📋 Export Sanitary Certificate
Official SENASA document for pet export
The CSE is issued by the official SENASA veterinarian certifying compliance with the sanitary requirements of the destination country.
SENASA · TUPA CA07 · Valid 10 days before shipment
See full definition →
📑 CVI — International Veterinary Certificate
Official health document · MERCOSUR model
Certificate for the international transit of dogs and cats, issued by the origin country's authority. Not to be confused with the U.S. CVI (USDA).
MERCOSUR · 60 days · Portaria MAPA 741/2024
See full definition →
📄 CVI (USA)
Certificate of Veterinary Inspection for US import
The CVI is the USDA-format sanitary certificate. Must be issued by SENASA-authorized vet and endorsed by USDA-APHIS via VEHCS.
VS Form 7001 · USDA endorsement · VEHCS · DMRVV
See full definition →
CDC Dog Import Form
Mandatory U.S. entry form
Free, online and one per dog. The receipt is valid only for the arrival date declared on it.
Free · 1 per dog

Form AC
Japan's certificate
No pencil, no correction fluid, no crossings-out. A typo can cost 180 days.
A typo = up to 180 days

European pet passport
EU standard model
Valid for life — but only issued to owners resident in the EU. It does not work for entering from a non-EU country.
Valid for life

ACF
CDC-registered animal care facility

🇺🇸 USDA-APHIS
US veterinary authority for pet importation
USDA-APHIS regulates animal importation to the US. Requires the CVI (VS Form 7001) endorsed by its official veterinarians.
7 CFR § 2.22 · VS Form 7001 · VEHCS · USA

CDC 2024 rule for dogs from rabies-endemic countries
Since August 2024, the CDC classifies Peru as a DMRVV country. Dogs exported to the US need CDC-approved lab vaccination or stay in rabies-free country.
89 FR 38450 · August 2024 · Peru = DMRVV · ACF

UK animal health authority post-Brexit

🇳🇿 MPI (New Zealand)
New Zealand animal biosecurity authority
MPI manages the world's strictest regime: FAVN 180 days prior + mandatory 10-day quarantine + MPI-accredited agent.
10-day quarantine · FAVN 180 days · Biosecurity NZ

🇨🇱 SAG (Chile)
Chile's sanitary authority for pet importation
SAG regulates pet importation to Chile. Accepts both FAVN and RFFIT ≥ 0.5 IU/mL and requires certificate in specific SAG format.

🇨🇦 CFIA (Canada)
Canadian federal agency for pet importation
Canada accepts FAVN or RFFIT ≥ 0.5 IU/mL. No quarantine if documentation is complete. Certificate must be in English or French.
FAVN or RFFIT · Certificate EN/FR · No quarantine · CBSA

🇨🇳 GACC (China)
Chinese customs authority for pet importation
The longest process: apostilled IVC + FAVN + 30-day quarantine. Minimum 4-6 months. Only through Beijing or Shanghai.
Apostilled IVC · FAVN · 30-day quarantine · 4-6 months · PEK/PVG

🇲🇽 SENASICA (Mexico)
Mexico's sanitary authority for pet importation
The most accessible Latin American destination. No mandatory FAVN, no quarantine. Just current rabies vaccine + bilingual certificate.
No FAVN · No quarantine · Current vaccine+ES-EN cert · CDMX

🇿🇦 DALRRD (South Africa)
South Africa's agricultural authority for pet importation
Mandatory 30-day quarantine in Johannesburg. FAVN must be at least 90 days old. Entry only through OR Tambo (JNB).
30-day quarantine · FAVN 90 days min · OR Tambo JNB

🇰🇷 APQA (South Korea)
South Korea's animal quarantine authority
With documented FAVN, quarantine is reduced from 180 to 7 days. Without it, the animal may spend 6 months in APQA facilities.
Quarantine 7-180 days per FAVN · Incheon · MAF QIA

🇪🇸 MAPA (Spain)
Spanish competent authority for imported pets
MAPA applies Regulation 576/2013 in Spain. Requires certificate in TRACES NT and inspection at the PCF of Barajas Airport.

Regulations and frameworks

🇪🇺 Regulation (EU) 576/2013
European legal framework for pet movement
Regulation 576/2013 establishes the 3 base requirements for bringing pets to the EU: ISO microchip + vaccine + FAVN ≥ 0.5 IU/mL + 3-month wait.
Art. 6 · FAVN+3 months · EU Passport · Third countries
See full definition →
🇪🇺 Regulation (EU) 2026/131
New EU pet travel framework (since 22 Apr 2026)
Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2026/131 completes the Animal Health Law (2016/429) and supersedes 576/2013. The substantive requirements —microchip, vaccine, FAVN ≥ 0.5 IU/mL and the 3-month wait— remain.
In force 22 Apr 2026 · 2016/429 · 2026/705 · 2026/636
View full definition →

Quarantine and timelines

Quarantine
Animal import quarantine
Mandatory isolation period imposed by the destination country. Ranges from 10 days (Australia) to 180 days (Japan) or ACF quarantine in the US.
10 days AUS · 180 days JPN · ACF USA

Up to 180 days confined, paid for by the owner. A faulty paper is enough.

Identification and fitness to fly

ISO Microchip
ISO 11784/11785 · FDX-B · 134.2 kHz
Mandatory electronic identification for pets. Must be implanted before the rabies vaccine for the health certificate to be valid.
ISO 11784/11785 · FDX-B

✈️ IATA Live Animals Regulations
International standard for live animal air transport
The IATA LAR sets minimum requirements for kennel, temperature, water and documentation for pet air transport in cargo.
Section 8 · CR82 dogs · CR83 cats · annual revision

🏥 Fit-to-fly certificate
Veterinary certificate of fitness for flight
Fit-to-fly certifies that the pet is clinically fit for air transport. Required by the airline, not the destination country.
Valid 7-10 days · Airline · Brachycephalic risk

Risk of reduced pressure in aircraft hold
Hypobaric conditions in the hold reduce available O₂ to ~75%. Especially dangerous for brachycephalic breeds and animals with cardiac conditions.
