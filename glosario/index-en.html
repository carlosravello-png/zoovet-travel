Pet export regulatory glossary

Technical and regulatory terms documented from real international pet export practice from Peru (2013-2026)

All terms

FAVN
🦠 Rabies
Mandatory notifiable zoonotic disease
The rabies virus is the root cause behind FAVN and all rabies vaccination protocols required in pet export.
Lyssavirus · OIE Lista A · Perú: vigilancia activa
View full definition →
📋 Export Health Certificate
Official SENASA document for pet export
The EHC is the document issued by the official SENASA vet certifying compliance with the destination country's health requirements.
SENASA · TUPA CA07 · Válido 10 días antes del embarque
View full definition →
🔬 RFFIT
Rapid Fluorescent Focus Inhibition Test
RFFIT measures rabies antibodies like FAVN but is not accepted by the EU, Japan, or Australia for pet import.
No válido UE · No válido Japón · No válido Australia
View full definition →
✈️ IATA Live Animals Regulations
International standard for live animal air transport
The IATA LAR sets minimum requirements for kennel, temperature, water and documentation for pet transport in aircraft hold.
Sección 8 · CR82 perros · CR83 gatos · revisión anual
View full definition →
🇺🇸 USDA-APHIS
US veterinary authority for pet import
USDA-APHIS regulates animal imports into the United States. It requires a CVI (VS Form 7001) endorsed by its official veterinarians.
7 CFR § 2.22 · VS Form 7001 · VEHCS · EE.UU.
View full definition →
🏛️ CDC US Import Rules
CDC 2024 rule for dogs from dog-rabies countries
Since August 2024 CDC classifies Peru as a DMRVV country. Dogs exported to the US need CDC-lab vaccination or stay in a rabies-free country.
89 FR 38450 · Agosto 2024 · Perú = DMRVV · ACF
View full definition →
🌐 OIE / WOAH
International animal health body
OIE/WOAH certifies FAVN as the reference method and maintains the list of globally recognized rabies labs, including KSVDL.
182 miembros · Certifica FAVN · Lista A rabia
View full definition →
🇪🇺 Regulation (EU) 576/2013
EU legal framework for pet movement
Regulation 576/2013 sets the 3 base requirements for bringing pets into the EU: ISO microchip + vaccine + FAVN ≥ 0.5 IU/mL + 3-month wait.
Art. 6 · FAVN+3 meses · Pasaporte EU · Países terceros
View full definition →
🇬🇧 APHA (United Kingdom)
UK post-Brexit animal health authority
APHA administers the GB Pet Travel Scheme. It adds tapeworm treatment and AHC to the standard FAVN requirements for pets from Peru.
GB Pet Travel Scheme · Tapeworm · AHC · post-Brexit
View full definition →
🇳🇿 MPI (New Zealand)
New Zealand animal biosecurity authority
MPI manages the world's strictest regime: FAVN 180 days prior + mandatory 10-day quarantine + MPI-accredited agent.
Cuarentena 10 días · FAVN 180 días · Biosecurity NZ
View full definition →
🇨🇱 SAG (Chile)
Chile health authority for pet import
SAG regulates pet import into Chile. It accepts both FAVN and RFFIT ≥ 0.5 IU/mL and requires the certificate in specific SAG format.
Res. N° 1547/2016 · FAVN o RFFIT · Lima–Santiago
View full definition →
🇪🇸 MAPA (Spain)
Spanish competent authority for imported pets
MAPA applies Regulation 576/2013 in Spain. It requires a TRACES NT certificate and inspection at the Barajas airport BCP.
FAVN+3 meses · TRACES NT · PCF Barajas · UE
View full definition →
🏥 Fit-to-Fly Certificate
Veterinary certificate of fitness for flight
The fit-to-fly certifies the pet is clinically fit for air transport. It is required by the airline, not the destination country.
Válido 7-10 días · Aerolínea · Braquicéfalos riesgo
View full definition →
🌡️ Hypobaric conditions in flight
Reduced pressure risk in aircraft hold
In-hold hypobaric conditions reduce available O₂ to ~75%. Especially dangerous for brachycephalics and animals with heart conditions on long-haul flights.
Presión 75% nivel del mar · O₂ reducido · Riesgo razas braquicéfalas
View full definition →
📄 CVI (United States)
Veterinary inspection certificate for US import
The CVI is the health certificate in USDA format. Must be issued by an authorized SENASA vet and endorsed by USDA-APHIS via VEHCS.
VS Form 7001 · USDA endorsement · VEHCS · DMRVV
View full definition →
Fluorescent Antibody Virus Neutralization
Rabies antibody titer test required to export pets to the EU, Japan, Australia, and other high-standard countries.
≥ 0.5 IU/mL · OIE/WOAH · KSVDL
Full definition →
ISO Microchip
ISO 11784/11785 · FDX-B · 134.2 kHz
Mandatory electronic identification for pets. Must be implanted before the rabies vaccine for the health certificate to be valid.
ISO 11784/11785 · FDX-B
Full definition →
SENASA
National Agricultural Health Service
Peruvian authority that issues the Export Health Certificate (CSE) required for all pet export processes from Peru.
DS 051-2000-AG · TUPA CA07
Full definition →
Quarantine
Animal import quarantine
Mandatory isolation period imposed by the destination country. Ranges from 10 days (Australia) to 180 days (Japan) or ACF quarantine in the USA.
10 days AUS · 180 days JPN · ACF USA
Full definition →
Rabies Serology
Rabies antibody titer test
Blood test measuring the level of antibodies against the rabies virus. FAVN is the internationally recognized OIE/WOAH methodology.
≥ 0.5 IU/mL · OIE/WOAH
