Regulation (EU) 576/2013: the rule governing pet export to Europe

Regulation 576/2013 establishes minimum requirements for bringing pets to the EU from Peru: ISO microchip + rabies vaccine + FAVN ≥ 0.5 IU/mL + 3-month wait from result date.

Author: Carlos Eduardo Ravello Joo Clinical review: Dra. Jessica Ysabel Camacho García, CMVP 12434 Published: 12 May 2026

Definition based on documentary practice 2013-2026 · Zoovet Travel, Trujillo, Peru

Quick definition

Reg. (UE) n.º 576/2013 · Art. 6 · microchip+vacuna+FAVN · Pasaporte EU

Regulation (EU) No 576/2013 is the EU rule governing movement of companion animals (dogs, cats, ferrets) within and into the European Union. It establishes three baseline requirements: ISO microchip, valid rabies vaccination, and for non-EU countries with rabies history, FAVN serology ≥ 0.5 IU/mL and 3-month wait.

Formal technical definition

Regulation 576/2013 repealed Decision 2003/803/EC. It applies to non-commercial movements of up to 5 animals. Its Annex II defines health certificate forms for third countries. Annex IV specifies rabies serology requirements: FAVN as the only valid method, OIE/WOAH-certified laboratory, result ≥ 0.5 IU/mL, at least 30 days after vaccination, and 3-month wait before traveling to the EU counted from the result date.

Regulatory context in export

To export a pet from Peru to Spain, France, Italy or another EU country, Regulation 576/2013 is the mandatory legal framework. The destination EU country may add additional controls but cannot reduce the Regulation's minimums. The Regulation also defines the health certificate format the SENASA vet must issue (Annex II, Part 3 for unlisted third countries).

Common errors

  • Not respecting the 3-month wait after FAVN result. A positive result does not authorize immediate travel. The 3-month wait is fixed under Regulation 576/2013.
  • Using the wrong certificate form. Regulation 576/2013 Annex II establishes the exact format. A generic SENASA CSE without the EU format is not accepted at EU border control.
  • Counting the 3 months from the vaccine instead of the result. The waiting period is counted from the FAVN result date, not the vaccination date.

Frequently asked questions

Does Regulation 576/2013 apply to the UK post-Brexit?
No. Since January 2021, the UK has its own pet travel scheme (GB Pet Travel Scheme) regulated by APHA. Requirements are similar but independent from the EU.
How many animals can I bring to the EU under Regulation 576/2013?
Up to 5 animals in non-commercial movements. More than 5 is considered commercial movement and different rules apply.
Does Regulation 576/2013 apply to all species?
It applies specifically to dogs, cats and ferrets. Other species (birds, rodents, reptiles) have different regulations.
Can I permanently move to Spain with my dog without FAVN?
No. FAVN with result ≥ 0.5 IU/mL and 3-month post-result wait are mandatory to bring a dog from Peru (unlisted EU country) into EU territory, regardless of whether it is tourism or permanent relocation.
Has Regulation 576/2013 changed since 2013?
It has been supplemented by implementing regulations and delegated acts. In 2021 the new Regulation (EU) 2016/429 (Animal Health Law) entered into force, coexisting with 576/2013 for the non-commercial pet regime.
