OIE/WOAH: international body that certifies FAVN and rabies laboratories

OIE/WOAH is the international animal health authority. It certifies FAVN as the reference method and maintains the globally recognized laboratory list, including KSVDL where Peruvian pet serology is processed.

Author: Carlos Eduardo Ravello Joo Clinical review: Dra. Jessica Ysabel Camacho García, CMVP 12434 Published: 12 May 2026

Definition based on documentary practice 2013-2026 · Zoovet Travel, Trujillo, Peru

Quick definition

OIE/WOAH · 182 miembros · FAVN certif. · Lista A rabia

OIE/WOAH (World Organisation for Animal Health) is the intergovernmental body setting health standards for international animal trade. It is the authority that certifies FAVN as the accepted rabies serology method and maintains the list of globally recognized OIE/WOAH laboratories.

Formal technical definition

Founded in 1924 as the Office International des Épizooties, OIE adopted the name WOAH in 2022. It groups 182 member countries. Its Manual of Diagnostic Tests and Vaccines for Terrestrial Animals is the normative reference for animal disease diagnostic methods. KSVDL is on the OIE/WOAH list of reference laboratories for rabies.

Regulatory context in export

OIE/WOAH is the primary validation source for FAVN. When the EU, Japan or Australia require an "OIE/WOAH-certified laboratory," they mean those on the official WOAH list. That certification is what makes KSVDL the reference laboratory from Peru.

Common errors

  • Confusing "accredited laboratory" with "OIE/WOAH-certified laboratory". Not every locally accredited lab is on the OIE/WOAH list.
  • Using results from a lab not on the OIE/WOAH list. The result is not internationally valid even if the method is correct.

Frequently asked questions

What is the OIE List A?
List A includes notifiable diseases of high transmissibility and severity. Rabies is an OIE List A disease.
How do I know if a lab is OIE/WOAH-certified?
At woah.int, Reference Laboratories section, the full list by disease is available. For rabies, KSVDL appears as a reference laboratory.
Are OIE and WOAH the same?
Yes. In 2022 OIE officially changed its name to WOAH (World Organisation for Animal Health) to better reflect its current mandate. Older documents use OIE; newer ones use WOAH. Same organization.
Does Peru report to OIE/WOAH?
Yes. Peru is an OIE/WOAH member with obligation to report listed diseases. SENASA is the national contact point.
Is FAVN the only method OIE accepts for rabies?
The OIE/WOAH Manual validates both FAVN and RFFIT, but most international trade regulations (EU, Japan, Australia) specify only FAVN.
