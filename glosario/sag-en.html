SAG (Servicio Agrícola y Ganadero): the health authority for importing pets into Chile

SAG regulates pet import into Chile from Peru. It requires microchip + vaccine + FAVN or RFFIT ≥ 0.5 IU/mL + SAG-format certificate. The Lima-Santiago route is the most frequent in South America.

Author: Carlos Eduardo Ravello Joo Clinical review: Dra. Jessica Ysabel Camacho García, CMVP 12434 Published: 12 May 2026

Definition based on documentary practice 2013-2026 · Zoovet Travel, Trujillo, Peru

Quick definition

SAG · Res. N° 1547/2016 · FAVN ≥ 0.5 · Ruta Lima–Santiago · Cuarentena domiciliaria

SAG (Servicio Agrícola y Ganadero) is the agency under Chile's Ministry of Agriculture responsible for animal health at borders. For importing dogs and cats from Peru, SAG requires rabies serology using FAVN or RFFIT (Chile accepts both, unlike the EU) with a result ≥ 0.5 IU/mL, plus an official health certificate issued by the official vet of the country of origin.

Formal technical definition

SAG Resolution N° 1547/2016 regulates the import of dogs and cats from countries with a rabies history. It requires: ISO microchip, valid rabies vaccination (at least 30 days before travel and no more than 1 year prior for first dose), FAVN or RFFIT rabies serology ≥ 0.5 IU/mL (samples at least 30 days post-vaccine), official health certificate from the country of origin in SAG format, and border inspection by SAG inspector.

Regulatory context in export

The Lima-Santiago route is the most traveled in South America for pet export. Chile, unlike the EU, accepts both FAVN and RFFIT, which simplifies laboratory costs. However, the health certificate must follow the exact SAG format — a standard SENASA certificate is not sufficient. SAG may require home quarantine post-entry if it detects irregularities in documentation.

Common errors

  • Presenting the standard SENASA certificate without adapting to SAG format. SAG has its own health certificate format. Presenting the standard SENASA format may result in border rejection.
  • Miscalculating the time between vaccine and serology sample. The sample must be taken at least 30 days after the vaccine. This period differs from the EU (which requires 21 days) — always verify current regulations.
  • Assuming Chile does not require serology because it is a neighboring country. Chile does require rabies serology for pets entering from Peru (a country with a rabies history). There is no exemption for geographic proximity.

Frequently asked questions

Does SAG accept RFFIT or only FAVN?
Unlike the EU, SAG accepts both FAVN and RFFIT. Both must give ≥ 0.5 IU/mL. This may be relevant if FAVN cost is a barrier for some families.
How far in advance of travel must serology be done?
The sample must be taken at least 30 days after the rabies vaccine. There is no minimum waiting period between the result and travel (unlike the EU which requires 3 months). Always verify current SAG regulations.
Is there mandatory quarantine when entering Chile with pets?
There is no mandatory quarantine for pets that meet all requirements. SAG may decree preventive home quarantine if there are doubts about documentation.
What happens if the pet does not have serology when crossing?
SAG may hold the animal in custody (costs at the owner's expense), require the test in Chile, and if not complied with, order return to the country of origin or euthanasia in extreme cases.
Can I take my pet from Lima to Santiago by bus or collective?
No. Pets can only enter Chile on commercial flights or in private vehicles through enabled border crossings with SAG inspector. International passenger buses are not authorized to transport animals.
