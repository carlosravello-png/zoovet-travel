MPI (Ministry for Primary Industries): the authority regulating pet import into New Zealand

MPI administers the world's strictest biosecurity regime. Importing a pet from Peru to New Zealand requires FAVN + 180 days + 10-day quarantine + multiple treatments, with a minimum 7-month lead time.

Author: Carlos Eduardo Ravello Joo Clinical review: Dra. Jessica Ysabel Camacho García, CMVP 12434 Published: 12 May 2026

Definition based on documentary practice 2013-2026 · Zoovet Travel, Trujillo, Peru

Quick definition

MPI · Biosecurity NZ · Cuarentena 10 días · País libre de rabia · FAVN obligatorio

New Zealand's MPI (Ministry for Primary Industries) administers the world's strictest biosecurity regime for imported pets. New Zealand is a rabies-free country and uses mandatory 10-day quarantine in MPI-approved facilities as the final health barrier, in addition to FAVN.

Formal technical definition

The pet import process to New Zealand requires: MPI application at least 6 months in advance, ISO microchip, two rabies vaccines with specific interval (primary + booster), FAVN ≥ 0.5 IU/mL at least 180 days before travel, specific antiparasitic treatments (Leishmania, Echinococcus, ticks) on exact dates, and 10-day quarantine at MPI facilities on arrival.

Regulatory context in export

Peru to New Zealand is the world's most complex pet export route from a country with a rabies history. MPI quarantine is not optional or negotiable: all dogs and cats from non-exempt countries must complete it. Quarantine costs are the owner's responsibility. MPI requires the entire process to be managed by an MPI-accredited Transitaire in the country of origin.

Common errors

  • Starting the process without 6 months lead time. MPI requires prior approval before starting the protocol. Without it, no prior steps are valid.
  • Not hiring an MPI-accredited agent. MPI requires the process to be managed by an accredited Transitaire in the exporting country. Failing to do so invalidates the process.
  • Confusing FAVN ≥ 0.5 with the 180-day period. For New Zealand, the positive FAVN must be at least 180 days old on the travel date. This is the longest deadline of all major destinations.

Frequently asked questions

How long does the complete import process to New Zealand take?
Minimum 7-8 months from the start of the protocol to travel. It is the longest process of all major destinations. The 10-day quarantine is added at the end.
Is KSVDL valid for the FAVN required by New Zealand?
Yes. KSVDL is on MPI's approved laboratory list for FAVN analysis.
How much does MPI quarantine cost?
Costs vary by animal weight and duration. Approximately NZD 1,500–3,000 per animal for the 10 days, plus handling and vet costs. The owner bears all costs.
Can I visit my pet during MPI quarantine?
Depends on the facility. Some MPI-approved facilities allow supervised visits. Check directly with the facility when booking.
Does New Zealand accept RFFIT instead of FAVN?
No. New Zealand specifically requires the FAVN (Fluorescent Antibody Virus Neutralisation Test), not RFFIT. The FAVN ≥ 0.5 IU/mL standard is mandatory.
