Frequently Asked Questions

Are these articles for general audiences or do they have scientific rigor?

They have scientific rigor. Each article includes up-to-date regulatory references — CDC, USDA APHIS, IATA, European Union regulations — and reviewed academic bibliography. They are designed for veterinarians needing export regulatory context and for technically informed owners who want to understand the rationale behind each procedure.

Who are the authors of the articles?

Jessica Ysabel Camacho Garcia (CMVP 12434), Veterinarian and Zootechnician from Universidad Privada Antenor Orrego, specialized in international pet travel medicine; and Víctor Jesús Camacho Paz (CMVP 3103), Veterinarian and Regional Zoonosis Coordinator for the La Libertad Region.

How often are new articles published?

The technical series is published on an ongoing basis. Each article addresses a specific topic in international pet travel medicine: rabies serology, quarantine, health certificates, microchip, metabolic stress during flight, brachycephalic breeds, hypobaric conditions, among others. You can subscribe via WhatsApp to receive notifications of new publications.

Can I share these articles with my regular veterinarian?

Yes, they are designed precisely for that use. If your veterinarian needs regulatory context about a specific destination or a procedure such as serology or the health certificate, the articles serve as updated regulatory reference material that can complement local clinical judgment.
